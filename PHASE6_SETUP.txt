BAIKU — PHASE 6
Cart + Checkout + Simulated Payment

WHAT WAS ADDED
- Logged-in customer carts synchronize with cart_items in MySQL.
- Guest carts remain in localStorage until sign-in.
- On sign-in, the local cart and saved database cart are merged.
- Cart quantities are capped by current product stock.
- Checkout requires an authenticated session.
- The server reads the authoritative cart from MySQL.
- The server recalculates prices and totals; the browser cannot choose the order total.
- Stock is checked and decremented in a database transaction.
- orders and order_items are created together.
- cart_items is cleared after a successful order.
- A simulated payment confirmation modal appears before the order is created.
- No real card, eSewa, Khalti, or bank transaction occurs.
- Header account text changes from “Sign in” to the logged-in customer's name and supports sign-out.

DATABASE
No SQL import is required. The existing baiku2_db schema already contains:
cart_items, orders, order_items, products, and users.

TEST CHECKLIST
1. Log in as your Phase 4 customer.
2. Add products to the cart.
3. Refresh the page; the cart should remain.
4. Check phpMyAdmin → baiku2_db → cart_items for the saved rows.
5. Change quantity/remove an item and confirm the database changes.
6. Open checkout.html. Customer name/email should be available.
7. Enter phone, address, city, and optional postal code.
8. Choose Cash on delivery or Demo card payment.
9. Click “Review simulated payment”, then “Confirm payment”.
10. Verify the #BK-#### confirmation.
11. Check baiku2_db → orders and order_items.
12. Confirm cart_items is empty for the customer.
13. Confirm product stock decreased by ordered quantities.
14. Open admin.php → Orders and verify the new order.
15. Change the order status and verify it persists.

IMPORTANT
This is a simulated payment flow for the college project. Do not enter real card numbers or financial credentials.
